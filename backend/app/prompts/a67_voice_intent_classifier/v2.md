## The classes — exactly one applies

- **`capture`** — a real household fact being reported, e.g. "Aarav has a
  field trip Friday." Routes to the Consequence Engine as a source.
- **`intention`** — a stated intention to do something later, e.g.
  "Remind me to call the plumber." Routes to Intention->Action.
- **`command`** — a direct, low-stakes instruction, e.g. "Mark the maid
  absent today." Report a `risk_tier` of `"R0"` (fully reversible,
  internal) or `"R1"` (reversible, visible) — never anything higher; a
  command that is genuinely higher-stakes than that is not a `command`,
  it is an `approval` or a `decision_trigger`.
- **`query`** — a real question about existing household state, e.g.
  "Did we pay the society dues?"
- **`approval`** — an explicit approval of a proposed external or
  financial action, e.g. "Yes, cancel that subscription." Report a
  `risk_tier` of `"R2"` (external communication, non-financial) or `"R3"`
  (any movement of money) — never anything lower.
- **`decision_trigger`** — a major, high-stakes household decision being
  raised, e.g. "Should we buy a car?" This always convenes the Household
  Council and is never resolved by voice alone — do not report a
  `risk_tier` for this class; it is set structurally elsewhere, not by
  you.
- **`correction`** — the speaker correcting a prior misunderstanding,
  e.g. "No, that's Kaku not Baba."
- **`ingest.scan_mailbox`** — a request to scan / check / read the
  household mailbox (Gmail, Outlook, "my inbox", "my email", "my mail")
  for recent messages, e.g. "Scan my Gmail and Outlook", "Check my inbox
  for the last 24 hours", "mera email scan karo", "go through my mail".
  This is a **read-only ingestion trigger** — it reads mail and files it
  into the household knowledge graph, nothing else. Do **not** report a
  `risk_tier` for this class; it carries none. Classify a turn here only
  when scanning the mailbox is plainly what is being asked — not for a
  turn that merely mentions an email in passing ("did you see Dad's
  email?" is a `query`).

## Two further judgments, honestly reported

- **`involves_hard_constraint_name`** — `true` only if a specific
  person's name is mentioned in a context where a real hard constraint
  (an allergy, a medical restriction, a custody rule) plausibly turns on
  correctly identifying exactly which person is meant. `false` otherwise
  — do not invent a hard-constraint concern that is not genuinely
  suggested by the turn.
- **`involves_staff_attendance_or_salary`** — `true` only if this turn is
  genuinely a staff attendance mark or a salary/advance entry.
