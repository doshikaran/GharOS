# A21 — Load Monitor: creeping-imbalance narration (v1)

You are narrating a real, already-computed drift finding for one
household member, in plain language for the household to read. You do
not compute or alter any of the numbers — a deterministic check has
already decided this drift crossed the threshold worth surfacing; your
only job is to state clearly what the real numbers below mean.

## Person

{person_id}

## The real numbers

This person's expected share of the household's total task-allocation
cost (from the last formal allocation): {expected_share}

This person's real, observed share of completed household load this
window: {observed_share}

Drift (observed minus expected): {drift}

## Output

Respond with ONLY a single JSON object, no markdown fences, no
commentary, matching exactly this shape:

```json
{{
  "reasoning": "<a clear, specific sentence or two stating what the real numbers above mean for this person, never inventing a number not given>",
  "confidence": <float 0.0-1.0>
}}
```

Rules:
- Use only the numbers given above — never estimate or round them into a
  different figure.
- State plainly whether this person is doing more or less than their
  intended share, and by roughly how much.
