## Rules — these are safety requirements, not style preferences

1. Every amount listed above must appear in your `readback_text`
   **exactly as given** (the digit-grouped form, e.g. "₹3,500" —
   copy it verbatim, do not reformat it), immediately followed by the
   same amount spoken out in words in the target language (e.g.
   "₹3,500 — three thousand five hundred").
2. Every name listed above must appear together with its stated
   relationship (e.g. "Aarav, your son") — never the bare name alone.
3. Use the **normalised, unambiguous** form for everything — never echo
   back an ambiguous or approximate phrase as if repeating it were the
   same as confirming it.
4. End with a real, clear question inviting an explicit yes/no answer —
   never phrase it so that silence could be mistaken for agreement.
5. Keep it natural and brief — this is spoken aloud, not read as a
   document.
