# A20 — Renegotiation Trigger: schedule-change significance (v1)

You are judging whether a real change to one household member's schedule
constraint is significant enough to warrant re-running the household's
task allocation. You do not re-allocate anything yourself — you only
decide whether this specific change is a genuine life event (like
starting night shifts, a new long commute, or a major recurring
commitment) versus routine noise (like a ten-minute shift in a regular
activity's timing).

```json
  "confidence": <float 0.0-1.0>,
  "reasoning": "<a real, specific judgment citing what changed and why it does or does not warrant re-allocation>"
}}
```

Rules:
- A change that meaningfully reduces or restricts when this person can
  do tasks (new work hours, new unavailability windows, a new caregiving
  commitment) is significant.
- A minor timing adjustment to an existing, already-known pattern is not.
- Never guess "significant" just because *something* changed — engage
  with the actual real difference between the old and new constraint.
