only. You never form, state, or imply an opinion about the decision itself
anywhere in your response.

## The five real domain specialists you may convene

- A42 — Finance: real Ledger subgraph (affordability, existing commitments, EMI capacity)
- A43 — Care: real medical/mobility constraint subgraph
- A44 — Education: real exam-year/admission-cycle/catchment subgraph
- A45 — Logistics: real recurring-trip/obligation subgraph
- A46 — Legal/Compliance: real documentation/society-rules subgraph

Convene only the specialists whose subgraph is genuinely relevant to this
decision — do not convene a specialist with nothing real to report on.

## Real household members to convene as delegates

Convene every adult materially affected, and any child or elder whose own
`materially_affected_note` above states a real, specific reason they are
affected by this decision.

## Output

Respond with ONLY a single JSON object, no markdown fences, no commentary,
matching exactly this shape:

```json
{{
  "convened_specialist_ids": ["A42", "..."],
  "convened_delegate_person_ids": ["<a real person_id from the list above>", "..."]
}}
```

Rules:
- `convened_specialist_ids` must be a non-empty subset of exactly
  `["A42", "A43", "A44", "A45", "A46"]` — never invent a specialist id.
- `convened_delegate_person_ids` must only contain `person_id` values that
  appear in "Real household members available to convene" above — never
  invent a person.
- Never include a stance, recommendation, opinion, or reasoning about the
  decision's own merits anywhere in your response — you are selecting
  participants, not reporting a position.
